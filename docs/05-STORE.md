## Yet Another Farming Station

**A greenhouse you place in one click, where the environment decides how fast things grow.**

Place the greenhouse, plant, plug in some water. It grows any crop that grows in stages, vanilla or
modded, and hands you the harvest at its output. Light, temperature and water set the pace; energy,
modules, fertilizer and soil push it further, each at a price.

### Highlights

- **Placed, not built.** One item, one click, and the whole volume appears. Three tiers that add
  room, never speed, and you upgrade in place without losing a thing.
- **Every crop, recognised.** The mod tries each seed on a virtual soil, so crops from other mods
  work with no configuration, and a data file can fix the ones that need it.
- **A readable environment.** Light, temperature and water are on screen, with a plain-language
  reason for whatever is holding a plot back.
- **Modules that stack.** Accelerators, density, grow lamp, heater, cooler, blackout, rain collector, battery, output capacity, composter.
  Nothing is free: whatever speeds things up also costs energy.
- **A resource loop.** Harvests leave compost, compost becomes fertilizer.
- **A base you can plug into anywhere.** Water, energy and fertilizer go in, the harvest comes out.
  Works with pipes, cables and hoppers from other mods.
- **Everything is data.** Crops, soils, fertilizers and modules are datapack files, and wherever
  items are named a `#tag` works. Server settings balance a whole modpack at once.

### Compatibility

Optional, never required: **JEI** (one page per crop), **Jade** (the greenhouse's state on hover)
and **Mystical Agriculture** (each crux seed needs its crux as a catalyst).

### Requirements

NeoForge for Minecraft 1.21.1.
