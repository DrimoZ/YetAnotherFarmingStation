# Yet Another Farming Station

A greenhouse you place in one click, where the environment decides how fast things grow. Place it,
plant, plug in some water: it grows any crop that grows in stages, vanilla or modded, and hands you
the harvest at its output. Light, temperature and water set the pace; energy, modules, fertilizer
and soil push it further, each at a price.

## What it does

- **Placed, not built.** One item, one click, and the whole volume appears. Three tiers that add
  room, never speed; you upgrade in place without losing a thing.
- **Every crop, recognised.** The mod tries each seed on a virtual soil, so crops from other mods
  work with no configuration, and a data file can fix the ones that need it.
- **A readable environment.** Light, temperature and water are on screen, with a plain-language
  reason for whatever is holding a plot back.
- **Modules that stack.** Nothing is free: whatever speeds things up also costs energy.
- **A resource loop.** Harvests leave compost, compost becomes fertilizer.
- **A base you can plug into anywhere.** Water, energy and fertilizer go in, the harvest comes out.
- **Everything is data.** Crops, soils, fertilizers and modules are datapack files.

The tables in this wiki are generated from the mod's data files and code, so every number quoted
