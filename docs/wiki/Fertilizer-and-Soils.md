# Fertilizer and Soils

## Residues and compost

A harvest sometimes leaves **compost**, with a chance per plant and per cycle. Four compost make a
**Fertilizer**, at a crafting table or with the Composter module. The loop is not free: compost
follows what the greenhouse produces, fertilizer use follows the number of plots. A small greenhouse
covers only a tiny part of its need; a greenhouse pushed to the limit makes more than it uses.

## Fertilizer

The **fertilizer slot** sits under the water gauge. It takes **one kind at a time**, which applies
to every plot that is growing: it speeds growth **or** raises the harvest.

<!-- gen:fertilizers -->
| Fertilizer | Effect on the whole greenhouse | One item feeds one plot for |
|---|---|---|
| Bone Meal | growth ×1.5 | 5 min |
| Fertilizer | harvest ×1.5 | 10 min |
<!-- /gen:fertilizers -->

Fertilizer is used **per growing plot and per unit of time**, never per plant or per speed: an
accelerated or dense greenhouse uses no more. An empty, switched-off or dry greenhouse uses none.
The number of items per minute is shown in the Operation tab.

## Soils

A plot's soil can yield more, **for that plot only**. This stacks with fertilizer, which applies to
the whole greenhouse. Hover a soil: its bonus is in its tooltip.

<!-- gen:soils -->
| Soils | Count as | Bonus on their plot |
|---|---|---|
| Fertile Soil | Farmland | harvest ×1.25 |
| Dirt, Grass Block, Dirt Path | Farmland | — |
