# Compatibility

Every integration is optional: the mod runs the same without them.

- **JEI**: a "Greenhouse crops" category, one page per crop (seed, soils, catalyst, harvest,
  requirements, duration, water, energy), with the three greenhouses as the machines.
- **Jade**: hover a block of the greenhouse to see its state, the plots that are growing, what
  limits them and the energy demanded.
- **Mystical Agriculture**: each crux seed needs its crux as a catalyst.
- **Pipes, cables and hoppers from other mods** plug into the base layer, on any side.
