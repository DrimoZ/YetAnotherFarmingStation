# Modules

A module goes into a slot of the Modules tab. A greenhouse accepts several modules of one kind, up
to its maximum, and **their bonuses add up** (two Accelerator I give +1 each on speed). Modules of
different kinds multiply with each other. The Grow Lamp and the Blackout exclude each other.

| Accelerator II | powered plots grow ×4 faster (bonuses add up) | plot energy ×16 | 16 |
| Accelerator III | powered plots grow ×8 faster (bonuses add up) | plot energy ×64 | 16 |
| Density I | +1 plant per plot | every plant drinks and uses energy | 16 |
| Density II | +2 plants per plot | every plant drinks and uses energy | 16 |
| Density III | +3 plants per plot | every plant drinks and uses energy | 16 |
| Battery | +1,000,000 FE of storage | — | 64 |
| Capacity | +64 items per output slot | — | 64 |
| Composter | turns 4 compost from the output into one fertilizer, 1 batch per step | — | 4 |
<!-- /gen:modules -->

Nothing is free: everything that speeds things up costs energy, and density multiplies water and
energy as much as it multiplies the harvest.

The number of module slots depends on the tier (see [Getting Started](Getting-Started#2-tiers)).
